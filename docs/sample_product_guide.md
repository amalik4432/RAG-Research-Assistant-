# Product User Guide - SmartHome Hub

## Introduction

Welcome to the SmartHome Hub! This guide will help you set up and use your new home automation system.

## What is SmartHome Hub?

SmartHome Hub is an intelligent home automation system that connects and controls all your smart devices from a single platform. With SmartHome Hub, you can control lights, thermostats, security cameras, door locks, and more.

## Key Features

### 1. Device Control
- Control up to 100 smart devices simultaneously
- Voice control integration with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Siri
- Mobile app for iOS and Android
- Web dashboard for computer access

### 2. Automation Rules
Create custom automation rules based on:
- Time of day
- Device status
- Location (home/away)
- Weather conditions
- Custom triggers

### 3. Energy Monitoring
- Real-time energy consumption tracking
- Monthly usage reports
- Smart recommendations to reduce energy costs
- Peak usage alerts

### 4. Security Features
- End-to-end encryption for all communications
- Two-factor authentication
- Activity logs and alerts
- Guest access with limited permissions

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Unbox Your Hub
Your package should include:
- SmartHome Hub device
- Power adapter
- Ethernet cable
- Quick start guide

### Step 2: Connect to Power
1. Connect the power adapter to the hub
2. Plug into a wall outlet
3. Wait for the LED to turn blue (about 30 seconds)

### Step 3: Download the App
- iOS: Search "SmartHome Hub" in the App Store
- Android: Search "SmartHome Hub" in Google Play Store
- Web: Visit app.smarthomehub.com

### Step 4: Create Your Account
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Create Account"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile

### Step 5: Connect Your Hub
1. Tap "Add Hub" in the app
2. Scan the QR code on the bottom of your hub
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect to Wi-Fi
4. Wait for the setup to complete (about 2 minutes)

## Adding Devices

### Adding Smart Lights
1. Make sure your smart bulb is powered on
2. In the app, tap "Add Device"
3. Select "Lighting"
4. Choose your bulb brand
5. Follow the pairing instructions
6. Name your light (e.g., "Living Room Lamp")

### Adding Thermostats
1. Your thermostat should be installed and powered on
2. In the app, tap "Add Device"
3. Select "Climate Control"
4. Choose your thermostat brand
5. Follow the pairing instructions
6. Configure heating/cooling preferences

### Adding Security Cameras
1. Install and power on your camera
2. In the app, tap "Add Device"
3. Select "Security"
4. Choose your camera brand
5. Scan the camera's QR code
6. Position and name your camera

## Creating Automation Rules

### Example: "Good Morning" Routine
1. Go to "Automations" in the app
2. Tap "Create New Automation"
3. Name it "Good Morning"
4. Set trigger: "Time" → 7:00 AM on weekdays
5. Add actions:
   - Turn on bedroom lights to 50%
   - Set thermostat to 72°F
   - Open bedroom blinds
   - Play morning playlist
6. Save the automation

### Example: "Away from Home" Routine
1. Create new automation
2. Name it "Away Mode"
3. Set trigger: "Location" → When I leave home
4. Add actions:
   - Turn off all lights
   - Set thermostat to eco mode
   - Lock all doors
   - Arm security system
   - Enable security camera notifications
5. Save the automation

## Troubleshooting

### Hub Won't Connect to Wi-Fi
- Make sure you're using a 2.4GHz network (not 5GHz)
- Check that your Wi-Fi password is correct
- Move the hub closer to your router
- Restart your router and try again

### Device Won't Pair
- Make sure the device is in pairing mode
- Check that the device is compatible with SmartHome Hub
- Verify the device is powered on
- Try resetting the device and pairing again

### App Shows "Offline"
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure the hub is plugged in and powered on
- Restart the hub by unplugging for 10 seconds
- Check for app updates

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use SmartHome Hub without internet?**
A: Basic local control will work, but cloud features like remote access and voice assistants require internet.

**Q: How many hubs do I need?**
A: One hub covers most homes up to 3,000 sq ft. Larger homes may benefit from additional hubs.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, all data is encrypted end-to-end and we never sell your personal information.

**Q: Can I control devices when I'm away from home?**
A: Yes, as long as your hub is connected to the internet, you can control devices from anywhere.

**Q: What happens if my internet goes down?**
A: Your automations will continue to work, but you won't have remote access until internet is restored.
