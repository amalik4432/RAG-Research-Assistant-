COMPANY POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
TechCorp Solutions Inc.
Last Updated: November 2024

================================================================================
1. REMOTE WORK POLICY
================================================================================

1.1 Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Managers may approve additional remote days on a case-by-case basis.

1.2 Equipment and Setup
- Company-provided laptop and monitor
- $500 annual stipend for home office equipment
- Ergonomic assessment available upon request
- IT support for home office setup

1.3 Communication Requirements
- Must be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Respond to messages within 2 hours during work hours
- Video on for all team meetings
- Update calendar with your working location daily

1.4 Security Requirements
- Use VPN for all company system access
- Secure home Wi-Fi with WPA2 or better
- Lock screen when away from computer
- No public Wi-Fi for accessing company systems

================================================================================
2. TIME OFF AND LEAVE POLICIES
================================================================================

2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Year 1: 15 days
- Years 2-5: 20 days
- Years 6+: 25 days
- Accrues monthly on a pro-rata basis
- Must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance
- Maximum carryover: 5 days to next year

2.2 Sick Leave
- 10 days per year, does not accrue
- No advance notice required (notify manager by 9 AM)
- Doctor's note required for absences over 3 consecutive days
- Can be used for family member illness

2.3 Holidays
Company observes 12 paid holidays per year:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve
- 2 Floating Holidays (employee choice)

2.4 Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Can be taken intermittently with manager approval

2.5 Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family: 5 days paid
- Extended family: 3 days paid
- Additional unpaid leave available upon request

================================================================================
3. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
================================================================================

3.1 Training Budget
Each employee receives an annual training budget:
- Individual Contributors: $2,000
- Senior/Lead roles: $3,000
- Managers: $4,000

Eligible expenses:
- Online courses and certifications
- Conference attendance
- Professional books and subscriptions
- Workshop and seminar fees

3.2 Conference Attendance
- Requires manager approval 30 days in advance
- Company covers registration, travel, and accommodation
- Must share learnings with team upon return
- Maximum 2 conferences per year per employee

3.3 Certification Reimbursement
- 100% reimbursement for job-related certifications
- Reimbursed upon successful completion
- Must maintain certification during employment
- If you leave within 12 months, pro-rated repayment required

3.4 Learning Time
- 4 hours per month for self-directed learning
- Schedule learning time with your manager
- Share monthly learning summary with team

================================================================================
4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
================================================================================

4.1 Salary Reviews
- Annual performance review in January
- Merit increases based on performance and market data
- Promotion reviews conducted quarterly
- Salary bands are transparent and available to all employees

4.2 Bonus Structure
- Annual performance bonus: 0-20% of base salary
- Company performance multiplier: 0.5x - 1.5x
- Paid in March following performance year
- Must be employed on payment date to receive

4.3 Equity Compensation
- Eligible after 6 months of employment
- Stock options vest over 4 years (25% per year)
- 90-day exercise window after leaving company
- Annual refresh grants for high performers

4.4 Health Benefits
- Medical, dental, and vision insurance
- Company pays 80% of employee premium, 50% of family
- HSA option with company contribution of $1,500/year
- FSA options for healthcare and dependent care

4.5 Retirement
- 401(k) with immediate enrollment
- Company matches 100% up to 6% of salary
- Immediate vesting of company contributions
- Financial planning services available

4.6 Other Benefits
- Life insurance: 2x annual salary (company paid)
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Gym membership reimbursement: $50/month

================================================================================
5. CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================

5.1 Workplace Behavior
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment
- Professional communication in all channels
- No alcohol or drugs on company premises or during work hours

5.2 Conflicts of Interest
- Disclose any outside employment or business interests
- No competing with the company
- No accepting gifts over $50 from vendors or clients
- Report any potential conflicts to HR immediately

5.3 Confidentiality
- All company information is confidential unless publicly disclosed
- No sharing proprietary information outside the company
- No discussing company matters on social media
- NDA remains in effect after employment ends

5.4 Social Media Guidelines
- Clearly state personal opinions are your own
- Don't share confidential company information
- Be respectful when discussing work-related topics
- Report negative comments about the company to communications team

================================================================================
6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

6.1 Goal Setting
- Quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
- Must align with team and company objectives
- Review progress in monthly 1-on-1s
- Update and adjust as needed

6.2 Performance Reviews
- Annual formal review in January
- 360-degree feedback from peers, manager, and direct reports
- Self-assessment required
- Development plan created for the coming year

6.3 Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
- Used when performance falls below expectations
- 60-90 day duration with clear goals
- Weekly check-ins with manager
- Successful completion removes PIP from record

6.4 Recognition Programs
- Peer recognition program (kudos points)
- Quarterly team awards
- Annual company awards with cash bonuses
- Spot bonuses for exceptional work ($250-$1,000)

================================================================================
7. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
================================================================================

7.1 Travel Expenses
- Book travel through company portal
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Standard hotel room (max $200/night in major cities)
- Rental car pre-approval required
- Submit expenses within 30 days of travel

7.2 Meals and Entertainment
- Client meals: Reasonable and customary
- Team meals: Requires manager approval
- No alcohol without manager approval
- Keep itemized receipts for all expenses over $25

7.3 Office Supplies
- Standard supplies ordered through company system
- Special requests require manager approval
- $100/year for personal workspace items
- IT equipment must be requested through IT department

7.4 Reimbursement Process
- Submit through expense system within 30 days
- Attach all receipts
- Provide business justification
- Reimbursement processed within 2 pay periods

================================================================================
8. TERMINATION AND RESIGNATION
================================================================================

8.1 Resignation
- Provide at least 2 weeks written notice
- Complete exit interview
- Return all company property
- Knowledge transfer to team required

8.2 Severance
- Voluntary resignation: No severance
- Layoff/reduction in force: 2 weeks per year of service (minimum 4 weeks)
- Termination for cause: No severance
- Severance includes COBRA contribution for coverage period

8.3 Final Paycheck
- Paid on normal pay cycle
- Includes unused PTO (not sick leave)
- Deductions for unreturned property or outstanding expenses
